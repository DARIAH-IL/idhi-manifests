Israeli Digital Humanities Index Schema

A LinkML schema for an index of Digital Humanities (DH) research in Israel. Entities and properties reuse established vocabularies (FOAF, schema.org, Dublin Core Terms, BIBO, DCAT, EDM, SKOS, CERIF); DH projects are classified with TaDiRAH 2.0 research-activity concepts (dynamic enum over the full taxonomy). Free text supports English, Hebrew and Arabic. All entities are identified by IDHI URNs (idhi::); external PIDs are supplementary.

URI: https://idhi.co.il/linkml/idhi

Name: idhi
